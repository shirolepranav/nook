# Nook & Cranny Support

Nook & Cranny keeps track of everything you own and where it is.
Need a hand? Email lightconeapps@gmail.com. We reply as soon as we can, usually within two business days.

When you write, it helps to include your iPhone or iPad model, your iOS version and the app version (Settings → Help & About → Version). Please don't send photos of your belongings or personal details unless we ask.

You can also reach us from the app: Settings → Help & About → Contact Us.

## Getting started

**How do I add a room's worth of things quickly?**
Tap the Capture button, choose Scan room, and photograph a shelf or a space. Tap each item in the photo to name it, then save. On devices with Apple Intelligence, the app suggests names for you. Everything works without it.

**How do I find something?**
Open the Find tab and type a name, a tag, a room or a spot. It finds things even if you misspell them. You can also ask a question such as "Where are my passports?" You can find or move items with Siri and Shortcuts too.

**How do I move something?**
Open the item and tap Move, then pick the room and spot. It takes two taps. The item keeps a history of where it has been.

## Warranties and lending

**How do warranty reminders work?**
Add the purchase date and warranty length (or attach the receipt). The app reminds you 30 days and 7 days before the warranty ends. Reminders need notifications to be allowed for the app in iOS Settings. They work offline.

**How do I keep track of things I've lent?**
Open the item, mark it as lent, and enter who has it and, if you like, a return date. Use the "Lent out" filter in Find to see everything that's out.

## Moving house

**How do box labels work?**
Open a box and choose its QR label. Print it or share it. Point the iPhone Camera at the label to open that box's contents. The label is saved to your Photos only if you choose to save it.

## Your data

**How do I move my home to a new iPhone?**
There are three ways, and you can use more than one:
- Set up the new iPhone from your old one or from a backup. iOS carries the app's data across.
- Turn on iCloud Sync (Nook & Cranny Pro).
- Make a backup file in Settings → Backup and keep it in Files or iCloud Drive. This is free, and it's a good extra copy.

**Is there an account? Does it need internet?**
No account and no internet are needed. Your data stays on your device. The only features that use the internet are buying Pro and iCloud Sync, both through Apple.

**I deleted something by accident.**
Tap Undo right after, or open Settings → Recently Deleted. Deleted items are kept there for 30 days.

**How do I keep some items private?**
Mark an item Private. Private items need Face ID or Touch ID to open and never appear in Spotlight, Siri or widgets. To lock the whole app, turn on the lock in Settings.

## Pro

**What's free and what's Pro?**
Free: unlimited rooms, search, backups, up to 25 items and 3 warranty reminders.
Nook & Cranny Pro is a one-time purchase. It unlocks unlimited items and reminders, insurance PDF and CSV export, and iCloud Sync. There is no subscription. Anything you've already added is never locked away.

**How do I restore my purchase or share it with my family?**
Open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases. Pro works with Family Sharing, so family members can use it from the same purchase.

**Does the app need Apple Intelligence?**
No. Every feature works without it. Where your device supports it, it just saves you some typing.

## Requirements

Nook & Cranny needs iOS 27 or iPadOS 27 and works on iPhone and iPad.
