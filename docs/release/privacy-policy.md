# Nook & Cranny Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date of release]

The short version: Nook & Cranny collects nothing. There's no account, no analytics and no ads, and what you add to the app stays with you.

## What stays on your device

Everything you add (items, photos, receipts, notes, rooms, spots, locations, and the people you've lent things to) is stored on your iPhone or iPad. We have no server, so we never receive it. Photos and text are read on your device, including when Apple Intelligence helps, and they are never sent to us or anyone else.

## iCloud Sync (Pro)

iCloud Sync is off until you turn it on. When it's on, your items sync through your own private iCloud database, which Apple encrypts and we can't see or access.

## Purchases

Buying Pro is handled by Apple. We don't see your name, payment details or Apple Account. Apple may share aggregated sales information with us, and crash reports from people who've chosen to share diagnostics with developers. These don't include what's in your app.

## Permissions

- **Camera:** to photograph your belongings. Photos stay on your device.
- **Face ID or Touch ID:** to lock the app and open Private items.
- **Photos (add only):** to save box labels you make. The app can't see the rest of your library.
- **Notifications:** for warranty and return reminders you set. They are created on your device.

## Sharing

Nothing is shared unless you share it. Reports, CSV exports and backup files are made on your device and leave it only through the share sheet, to wherever you choose.

## Private items

Items you mark Private need Face ID or Touch ID to open and never appear in Spotlight, Siri or widgets.

## Tracking and children

We don't track you, we don't use third-party advertising or analytics, and the app contains no ads. Nook & Cranny isn't aimed at children, and because it collects no data, it collects none from children either.

## If you email us

If you write to lightconeapps@gmail.com, we get your email address and what you write. We use it only to reply to you, and we don't share it.

## Deleting your data

Delete an item and it goes to Recently Deleted for 30 days, then it's removed. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. If you used iCloud Sync, you can remove its data in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. Backup files you've saved are yours to delete.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
